FAQs

Commercial buying, answered

Still unsure? Our advisors are happy to help, with no obligation.

What rental yield can I expect?

Grade-A offices and high-street retail in Delhi NCR usually yield 6–9% a year, depending on the location, tenant and lease terms.

What is an SCO plot?

A shop-cum-office plot lets you build a multi-floor building with retail on the ground floor and offices above. You own the land and can use, sell or lease each floor.

Do you help find tenants?

Yes. Our leasing team helps find and vet tenants, negotiate lease terms and handle the paperwork.

Is GST applicable on commercial property?

GST at 12% applies on under-construction commercial units. Ready-to-move units with a completion certificate are exempt.
