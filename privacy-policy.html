Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Investors Planet Realty (“we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy. This policy sets out what we collect when you use this website or contact us, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to this website and to enquiries made through it.

Who We Are

We are a real estate agent registered with the Uttar Pradesh Real Estate Regulatory Authority under registration number UPRERAAGT000235/11/2025, with our office at G-08, D-216, Block-D, Sector-63, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201301. We market and advise on residential and commercial projects built by third-party developers. We are the party responsible for the information described in this policy.

Information We Collect

  1. Information you give us. When you submit an enquiry form, request a call back or contact us through WhatsApp, we collect your name, phone number, email address, city and the property type or specific project you are enquiring about, together with anything else you choose to tell us about your requirement.
  2. Information your visit provides. Our servers record standard technical information sent by your browser, including IP address, device and browser type, the pages you view and the time of your visit. We use this in aggregate to understand which pages are useful.
  3. Information we do not collect here. We do not request financial details, identity documents or payment information through this website. Information of that kind is collected only later, in person or by the developer, as part of an actual booking.

How We Use Your Information

  1. To respond to you. To call, message or email you about the enquiry you have made.
  2. To advise you. To shortlist projects matching the budget, location and property type you have described, and to arrange site visits.
  3. To register your enquiry. To introduce you to the developer of a project you are interested in, so that your visit or booking is recorded against your name.
  4. To meet our obligations. To keep records of our dealings with you as required of a registered real estate agent.
  5. To improve this website. To understand, in aggregate, how the site is used.

How We Share Your Information

We are an agent, not the developer, and this affects how your information moves. Please read this clause carefully.

  1. With developers. When you enquire about a specific project, we share your name and contact details with the developer or promoter of that project. This is how a site visit or booking is registered against your enquiry. By submitting an enquiry, you ask us to do this.
  2. With service providers. With the providers who operate our hosting, email and call handling, strictly to the extent needed to provide those services to us.
  3. Where the law requires it. Where we are obliged to disclose information by law, by a court, or by a regulator such as UP RERA.

We do not sell your information, and we do not pass it to unrelated brokers or to marketing lists.

Calls, Messages and Consent

By submitting an enquiry you request contact from us, and you agree that we and the developer of the project concerned may call, WhatsApp, SMS or email you in response. This request operates as your consent notwithstanding any registration of your number under the National Do Not Call or Do Not Disturb registries, for the purpose of that enquiry only. You may withdraw it at any time by telling the person who contacts you, or by writing to us at the address in clause 12, and we will stop.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website uses cookies and similar browser storage for basic functionality and for anonymous traffic measurement. You may block or delete cookies through your browser settings. The site will continue to work, although some preferences will not be remembered between visits.

How Long We Keep Your Information

We retain enquiry records for as long as we are in contact with you regarding a property, and thereafter for the period necessary to meet our record-keeping obligations as a registered agent. Where neither purpose applies, we delete the record.

How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information you give us, including restricting access to those who need it. No transmission over the internet can be guaranteed entirely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security; where an incident affects your information, we will inform you promptly.

Your Rights and Choices

  1. Access. You may ask us what information we hold about you.
  2. Correction. You may ask us to correct anything that is inaccurate or out of date.
  3. Deletion. You may ask us to delete your information. Where we are obliged to retain a record, we will tell you what we are keeping and why.
  4. Withdrawal of consent. You may ask us to stop contacting you at any time.

To exercise any of these, write to info@investorsplanet.co.in or call +91 9555891638. We will act on your request without undue delay.

Links to Other Websites

Where we link to a developer’s website or to a government portal, that site is governed by its own privacy practices. We are not responsible for the content or the practices of any site we do not operate.

Changes to This Policy

We may revise this policy from time to time. The version in force is the one published on this page, and its date appears above. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
