Privacy Policy

What we collect when you enquire about a property, why we collect it, who we pass it to, and how to ask us to change or delete it.

Last updated 30 September 2026

Investors Planet Realty (“we”, “us”) is a RERA-registered real estate agent operating from Noida, Uttar Pradesh. This policy explains what we collect when you use this website or contact us, why we collect it, who we share it with, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Registered agent number UPRERAAGT000235/11/2025. Office: G-08, D-216, Block-D, Sector-63, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201301.

What we collect

We only ask for what we need in order to call you back about a property.

  • What you give us. Your name, phone number, email address, city and the property type or project you are enquiring about, submitted through an enquiry form, a call-back request or the WhatsApp button.
  • What your visit tells us. Standard technical information your browser sends, such as IP address, device and browser type, pages viewed and the time of your visit. This is used in aggregate to understand which pages are useful.

We do not ask for financial details, identity documents or payment information through this website. Anything of that kind is collected later, in person or through the developer, as part of an actual booking.

Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry by phone, WhatsApp or email.
  • To shortlist projects that match what you have told us about budget, location and property type.
  • To arrange site visits and, where you ask us to, to introduce you to the developer of a project.
  • To keep a record of our correspondence with you, and to meet our obligations as a registered real estate agent.
  • To improve the website.

Who we share it with

This is the part most worth reading. We are an agent, not the developer. When you enquire about a specific project, we share your name and contact details with the developer or promoter of that project, because that is how a site visit or a booking is registered against your enquiry. By submitting an enquiry you are asking us to do this.

Beyond that, we share your information only with:

  • Service providers who run things on our behalf, such as hosting, email and call handling, and only to the extent they need it.
  • Anyone we are required to disclose to by law, by a court, or by a regulator such as UP RERA.

We do not sell your data, and we do not pass it to unrelated brokers or marketing lists.

Calls and messages

When you submit an enquiry you are asking us to contact you, and you agree that we or the developer may call, WhatsApp, SMS or email you about it. This holds even if your number is registered on the National Do Not Call register, because your enquiry is the consent. You can withdraw it at any time by telling the person who calls, or by writing to the address at the end of this page, and we will stop.

Cookies

This site uses cookies and similar storage for basic functionality and for anonymous traffic measurement. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work, though some preferences will not be remembered between visits.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry records for as long as we are in touch with you about a property, and afterwards for as long as we need to in order to meet our record-keeping obligations as a registered agent. When neither applies any more, we delete it.

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you give us, including restricting who can see it. No website or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security, but we will tell you promptly if something happens that affects you.

What you can ask us to do

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct anything that is wrong, delete it, or stop contacting you. Write to info@investorsplanet.co.in or call +91 9555891638 and we will act on it. Where we are obliged to retain a record, we will tell you what we are keeping and why.

Links to other sites

Where we link to a developer’s website or a government portal, that site has its own privacy practices and we are not responsible for them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will appear on this page with a new date at the top. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
