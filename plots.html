FAQs

Plots buying, answered

Still unsure? Our advisors are happy to help, with no obligation.

Can I get a loan for a plot?

Yes. Banks offer plot loans of up to 70–75% of the value, and a composite loan if you plan to build within a set period.

Is there a deadline to start construction?

Some townships require construction to begin within a few years of possession. We explain every condition before you book.

What should I check before buying land?

Title documents, approved layout, land-use conversion, RERA registration, encumbrance certificate and road access. We verify all of these for you.

Are farmhouse plots legal?

Farmhouse plots are legal where the land use allows it. We only recommend projects with the necessary approvals.
