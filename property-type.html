FAQs

Residential buying, answered

Still unsure? Our advisors are happy to help, with no obligation.

Should I buy ready-to-move or under-construction?

Ready-to-move homes let you move in immediately with no GST, while under-construction homes offer lower prices and flexible payment plans. We help you weigh both for your budget and timeline.

What costs are there beyond the base price?

Expect stamp duty and registration (about 5–7%), GST on under-construction homes (5%), club membership, parking and maintenance deposits. We share a complete cost sheet upfront.

How much home loan can I get?

Banks usually fund 75–90% of the property value, depending on your income and the loan amount. Our experienced team can walk you through the options and the paperwork.

Can NRIs buy residential property?

Yes. NRIs can buy residential property in India freely. Our experienced team is here to help you every step of the way, wherever you are based.
