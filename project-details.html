Project Description

A luxury retail destination on a 300-metre frontage in Sector 72, Noida. The linear structure carries double-height stores at ground level, three floors of anchor stores behind a prime façade, a hypermarket on the lower ground, and dining from a food court through to alfresco and fine dining on the upper levels.

Every detail, from the layouts to the landscaping, has been designed around how families actually live: privacy where you want it, community where you need it and nature all around.

M3M The Line sits on Plot MPC-01 in Sector 72, Noida, Gautam Budh Nagar, with a 300-metre frontage along the main road.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is M3M The Line located?

M3M The Line is located at Sector 72, Noida, with excellent connectivity to NH-48 & Dwarka Expressway and Rapid Metro / Yellow Line.

What is the starting price of M3M The Line?

Prices start from ₹ 28,000/sq.ft* on the upper floors, rising to ₹ 49,000/sq.ft* for a double-height ground-floor store. Contact us for the latest price list, offers and payment plans.

Which configurations are available?

M3M The Line is a retail and commercial development over a lower ground floor plus five levels, with anchor stores, a hypermarket and dining floors. Unit sizes are confirmed on enquiry

Is M3M The Line RERA approved?

Yes. The project is registered under RERA with registration number RERA-GUR-101-2026.

What is the possession date?

Possession is expected by Dec 2029, as per the RERA timeline.

Do you charge brokerage for this project?

No. As an authorised channel partner, we offer the official developer price with zero brokerage on primary bookings.
