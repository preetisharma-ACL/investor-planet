Project Description

The Crest Residences by Premier Developers is a thoughtfully planned residential community at Sector 65, Golf Course Ext. Road, Gurugram. Spread across 6.8 acres, it brings together 3 & 4 BHK residences with generous open spaces and superb connectivity.

Every detail, from the layouts to the landscaping, has been designed around how families actually live: privacy where you want it, community where you need it and nature all around.

Residents enjoy quick access to NH-48 & Dwarka Expressway and Cyber City & Golf Course Road, with schools, hospitals and shopping all within a short drive. Every home is Vastu-compliant and backed by transparent pricing and a clear title.
